# Product

## Register
product (design serves the tool)

## What it is
GymHub is a personal gym and nutrition tracker. One user, phone only, local-first. It logs sets, meals, water and weight, and derives every stat from what was logged.

## Who uses it and when
A lifter between sets, one hand on the phone, gym lighting with glare, often sweaty and rushed. Logging must take seconds. Tap targets are large and text is readable at arm's length.

## Personality
Physical, precise, a little heavy. The interface feels like gym equipment: solid, tactile, no fluff. 3D is used where it carries information (muscle heat, water level, load), not as decoration.

## Anti-references
Generic AI-built app looks: purple-blue gradients, gradient text, glass cards, icon-box rows, eyebrow badges, Inter, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif, fade-in-on-scroll, grain over gradient, lucide icons, unstyled shadcn look, em dashes, buzzword copy.

## Principles
1. Logging speed beats visual effect. Frequent actions get no animation.
2. Every 3D element encodes real logged data and has a flat, accessible fallback.
3. High contrast always. Body text at least 4.5:1.
4. One spacing scale, one accent color, used with intent.
5. Motion is short, ease-out, interruptible, and respects reduced motion.
